QAMart POSAPI documentation and business rules

Overview

Base URL: . Requests and responses use JSON. Amounts are integers in Indonesian rupiah (IDR).

Every endpoint except /api/health, /api/test/reset and /api/auth/login needs Authorization: Bearer <token>. Tokens are valid for 1 hour and must stop working after logout.

Performance requirement: every endpoint must respond in under 1000 ms.

Error shape:

{ "error": { "code": "VALIDATION_ERROR", "message": "Some fields are invalid.", "details": { "price": "..." } } }
StatusMeaning
400Validation error, invalid JSON, invalid / expired promo code, promo minimum not met

Business rules

Roles

  • Cashier: makes sales, sees and opens only their own transactions.
  • Manager: everything a cashier can do, plus sees all transactions, voids transactions, and creates or updates products.
  • Email at login is case-insensitive and surrounding spaces are ignored. Suspended accounts get a 403 with a clear message.

Cart and stock

  • Quantity per line: 1-99, and never more than the stock available. A product with stock 0 cannot be sold.
  • A completed sale reduces stock by the quantity sold. A voided sale puts the stock back.

Price calculation

  1. subtotal = sum of price × qty.
  2. discount from the promo code (if any).
  3. tax (PPN 11%) = round((subtotal − discount) × 0.11) to the nearest rupiah.
  4. total = subtotal − discount + tax.

Codes are case-insensitive. Only one promo code per transaction.

Payment

  • cash: amountPaid is required and must be greater than or equal to the total. change = amountPaid − total.
  • card and qris: the exact total is charged. amountPaid is optional; if sent it must equal the total.

Void

  • Managers only. A reason of 5-200 characters is required. A transaction can be voided once (409 after that).
